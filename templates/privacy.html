Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Your financial information is personal. This Privacy Policy explains what data Spendly collects, why we collect it, and how we keep it safe.

1. Data We Collect

We collect only what we need to run the service:

  • Account information: your name, email address and password (stored as a secure hash, never in plain text).
  • Expense data: the amounts, categories, dates and descriptions you enter.
  • Basic technical data: information such as browser type and session cookies needed to keep you signed in.

We do not collect bank account details, card numbers or access to your financial institutions.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Store and display your expenses, summaries and category breakdowns.
  • Keep the service secure and fix problems.
  • Contact you about important changes to the service or these policies.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use your expense data for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in a secured database. We take reasonable technical measures to protect it from unauthorised access, loss or misuse, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal information and expense records are removed.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal or expense data with third parties for their own purposes. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive basic technical information such as your IP address when your browser requests them. If we add other service providers in future, such as hosting or email delivery, they will process data only on our behalf and we will update this policy.

5. Your Rights

You can view, edit or delete your expenses at any time, and you can ask us to delete your account and all associated data.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact the Spendly team. See also our Terms and Conditions.
